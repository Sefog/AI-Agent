CUSTOMER SUCCESS ANALYSIS

A text box where one can paste a customer email or support ticket.

Analyze the customer's sentiment (positive, neutral, negative).

Identify the main problem.

Assess customer urgency (low, medium, high).

Estimate churn risk (low, medium, high).

Recommend what the Customer Success Manager should do next.

Draft a professional response to the customer.

CUSTOMER DATA ANALYSIS

Allows pasting of sample customer data.

Identifies trends and patterns.

Highlight potential customer retention problems.

Summarizes important business findings.

Provides practical recommendations.
